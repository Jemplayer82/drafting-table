    Ideas & direction

    • Strong pull toward outdoor/nature photography as the hero, not stock-flat illustration — 7 of 9 site-screenshot pins lead with a full-bleed landscape or macro-nature shot.
    • Dark or deep-green grounds dominate (6 of 9 screenshot pins) over plain white — palette direction likely leans dark, with one warm accent doing all the work (gold, lime, copper, or rust — never more than one per site).
    • Numbered process/step markers show up twice (MNTN, Dirt Days) as a way to walk through content without a traditional nav-heavy structure.
    • Stat bars (years / projects / clients / awards) appear on 3 of 9 — a recurring trust-building device worth considering for credibility sections.
    • Serif-display + clean-sans-body pairing repeats across several (MNTN, BALI, Leo Mercer) — reads as the throughline typographic direction rather than a one-off.
    • The board pivoted hard to palette-hunting on the second pass: all 9 newly added pins are standalone color-palette cards, not site screenshots — this project is now clearly in the "lock the palette" phase.
    • Across all 10 palette pins (the original gold/teal one plus 9 new), dark teal / navy / near-black is the single most repeated base — it shows up as the dominant or secondary tone in 8 of 10 palettes (BYQT, Casa Chromatica, tammythree, Midnight Tide, Stone/Lagoon, Van Dyke, fog-forest, Sea Glass). Sage/olive-green (from the original screenshot pins) barely reappears here — teal has clearly overtaken green as the leading "dark ground" candidate.
    • Only one new palette (Casa Chromatica, Coral Ember) commits to a single hard accent the way the original gold/teal pin did; the rest lean on a full neutral ladder (near-black → mid-tone → sand/pale) with no single pop color. Two independent competing directions now: "dark ground + one loud accent" vs. "dark-to-light neutral gradient, no accent."
    • Two of the new pins (Stone/Lagoon/Dune/Reef/Cliff and Sea Glass/Beach Mist/Current/Tidal Wave/Coastal Slate) both label their swatches 01 Main / 02 Secondary / 03–05 Additional — worth adopting that naming convention for whichever palette we land on, independent of which colors win.

    Open questions

    • Which single accent color should carry the identity — gold/amber, coral/rust, or none at all? The original palette pin voted gold; the new batch is split between one coral-accent palette (Casa Chromatica) and eight accent-free neutral-ladder palettes. Gold no longer looks like the clear leader — needs a real decision, not just more pins.
    • Teal/navy vs. green as the dark ground color: early screenshot pins leaned olive/forest, but 8 of the 9 new palette pins are teal- or navy-based. Has the direction actually shifted, or were these saved just to compare options?
    • Full dark theme throughout, or dark hero fading to a lighter body like Wild Light Photography and BALI do?
    • Is the numbered-step device meant to structure real content (an actual process) or just used for visual rhythm — worth confirming before adopting it.
    • Adopt the "01 Main / 02 Secondary / 03-05 Additional" labeling convention from the Stone/Lagoon and Sea Glass palettes for our own final palette once it's chosen?
